About Zannoo

A school where every child is known by name

At Zannoo International School we believe each child has a gift worth discovering. Small classes and dedicated teachers mean we notice how every pupil learns, and we shape our teaching around them.

We combine the strengths of the Ghanaian curriculum with international best practice, so pupils leave us ready for the BECE, ready for senior high, and ready to take their place in a connected world.

“Knowledge, Character, Excellence.”

Academic excellence

High expectations, clear progress tracking, and regular feedback to parents.

Discipline & character

Respect, honesty and responsibility are taught every day, not just talked about.

International mindset

Pupils learn about other cultures and languages while staying proud of their own.

Safe & caring campus

A secure compound, trained staff and a nurse on site for peace of mind.

Admissions

Joining Zannoo in four simple steps

  1. Send an enquiryFill in the form or call the admissions office.
  2. Visit the schoolTour the campus and meet the head teacher.
  3. Entrance assessmentA friendly, age-appropriate assessment for Basic 1 and above.
  4. Receive your offerComplete registration, collect uniforms and books, and you're in.

Please bring your child's birth certificate, two passport photos, the last school report (if any) and immunisation records.
